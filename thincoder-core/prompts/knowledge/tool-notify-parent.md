- Delivery markers: the message your parent receives is headed `[上抛·待裁]` (ask - awaiting the parent's decision) or `[上抛·知会]` (note - FYI). They are added for you - do not write these markers inside `message`. In your final report the marker is yours to write: tag items still awaiting the parent's decision `[上抛·待裁]`, FYI items `[上抛·知会]`.
- NON-BLOCKING: it returns immediately. Keep working on the unaffected parts and leave the affected part pending; no fetch, no polling. Finishing first means reporting the unanswered part as not done.
- SYNCHRONOUS SPAWN: if the parent is blocked on your run, nothing can be sent back - `send` reaches only a RUNNING ASYNC child, and the message is read when the parent's call returns (it may re-spawn you with an answer). Do not idle-wait.
- Out of scope: naming / implementation / wording details, anything a read or a command would answer, a trade-off the task book already states - those follow the stop-and-report discipline, not this channel. A confirmation-type question (asking for a nod before you continue) does not belong here either: the parent cannot approve anything, so the nod buys nothing - decide, or carry the question to the user-facing seat through your report.
- Availability: subagents only (depth > 0). At depth 0 you talk to the user through your normal reply or the `question` tool.
- Limits: `message` is at most 1,500 characters, and the parent's in-flight queue holds at most 20 messages - exceeding either is an explicit error, never a silent drop.
