Three steps: `append` (add a round to the END of §3), `edit` (replace one anchored range inside it), `status` (your section's status line).

The record is the one bound to this review instance; its path arrives with the instance key, so there is no path parameter.

- `append` — a design-review round belongs here: one row per finding (`#` / severity / status / evidence) where the evidence cell holds a pointer — a section number, a symbol name, or the exact words of the line — plus the VERDICT line and the counts. The report's own paragraphs stay out of the record. The round heading (`### 轮次 N（评审子代理）`) is generated by the tool — do not write your own; it would be dropped. Limit 20000 characters per call. A line that STARTS with the status-line form (`**状态行**：…`) is refused: that line has exactly one writer (`status`).
- `edit` — `oldText` must match exactly once, byte for byte, inside §3; `newText` replaces it (empty `newText` deletes it). Refused with nothing written: a blank `oldText`, zero hits, two or more hits, an anchor crossing the section boundary, an anchor touching the protected status line, and a replacement that would introduce a status line or leave more of them than before.
- `status` — `value` carries exactly ONE legal keyword of §3's own word list (`评审通过`) plus decoration (surrounding symbols, a trailing ISO date, whitespace); prose goes to `note` and lands as a parenthetical.

`segment` names the section you are writing — `"§3"` is this face's pen. Everything else on the record belongs to another author: §1 discussion, §2 design, §4 user approval, §5 implementation record and §6 verification are written by their own seats, and writing them is refused.

A finding enters the record only with a pointer that resolves — an evidence cell that points at nothing is a reading, not proof that the thing exists.
